The pool degrades instead of trapping: a failure at slot 0 traps, any later slot
just shrinks `dat_149c3d660` to what was created, so the count is the real
number of live workers (five in the normal case).

## Evidence

`dat_149c3d660 = 5` is written before the loop and is the loop bound, the
`0x40` record stride matches `0x043c4140`'s `slot * 0x40` indexing, and
`0x043c4140` is passed as the thread start. `0x1419ecd00` is a thin
`CreateEventA(0,0,0,0)` (`0x1419ec680 + 0x680`).

## Codegen

Drafted from the disassembly; the clamp-on-failure and the 0x30 stride of the
queue loop are the parts to keep.
